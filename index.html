01 — About

A little background

I'm a software engineer with five years of experience turning fuzzy problems into products people actually use. Most of my work sits where interface design meets systems thinking — the part where a good decision early saves a hundred small ones later.

I started out writing internal tools, which taught me that the best software disappears: it does the job, then gets out of the way. These days I work mostly in TypeScript and Python, and I have a soft spot for performance work and for making things usable with a keyboard and a screen reader.

Outside of work you'll find me reading, running slowly, and maintaining a few small open-source projects. I'm always happy to talk about a problem worth solving.
